# Initial Domain Capability Map

**Version:** 1.0  
**Status:** Pending Review  

This document serves as an **initial capability inventory** to support the business domain model. It breaks down each business domain into its major business capabilities. These capabilities represent stable business abilities the domain possesses, not detailed technical implementations, process models, or agent definitions.
